# The pixel face

`W95FA.otf` is W95FA by FontsArena, a redrawing of an 8-point bitmap sans, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`LICENSE-W95FA.txt`). `pixel-20*.bin` (regular) and `pixel-23*.bin` (bold) are PocketJS font atlases baked from it by `tools/pixel-font.ts` at raster densities 1 to 4; they are derived from the font and are under the same licence.
